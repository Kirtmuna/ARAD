## What is this Mod?

ARAD is an unofficial extension mod for RealTrainMod (RTM), NGTLib, and MCTE.

## Notes & Precautions

**We accept no responsibility or liability for any issues caused by using this mod.**
**KaizPatchX-1.10.1 or later is required.**
**Original RTM may work as well, but compatibility is not verified.**

**[PR] Installing CrossTie-1.0.0-Alpha11 or later is recommended for better performance and experience.**
[Download CrossTie from here](https://github.com/suzumiyatrainer/CrossTie)

## How to Install

Download this mod and place it into your `mods` folder.

## How to Use

First, as a prerequisite, a map feature is available, and two new blocks are added: Station Block and Speed Limit Block.
By setting a train set item generated by the built-in ARTPE system (from AppleExtended) into a Station Block, and selecting stations in order from the map to create a route, the train set stored in the initial Station Block will spawn and stop at each station sequentially until reaching the final terminus.

## Helpful Tips & Known Characteristics

- This mod performs per-tick calculations on every train set, which may lead to noticeable TPS drops depending on your environment.
- The processing mechanism in this mod does not track or understand the physical concept of "rails." Therefore, if a train diverges off-path, the mod cannot automatically adjust or rectify its direction.
  *(This design decision prevents heavy pre-loading processes along upcoming tracks, which would otherwise severely reduce TPS).*
- Currently, train sets spawn at the starting station and despawn upon reaching the terminal station. If this affects immersion, we recommend placing starting and terminal stations inside train depots/yards.
